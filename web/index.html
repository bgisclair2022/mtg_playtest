      Smarter Jace (Claude)

      Once per turn of Jace's, and at its mulligan, Claude reads the game and tells Forge's AI what to cast first, what to hold back and how to attack. Forge still plays the cards; if Claude is slow or fails, Jace plays as usual. Only in-app games use it, not simulations.

      It also decides whether to answer your spells, and learns: after each game Claude reviews the log, grades Jace's choices and rewrites data/bot-lessons.md, which every later decision reads.
