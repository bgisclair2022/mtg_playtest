## How to play

Play tight, careful Magic.

- Sequence spells to use all of your mana each turn.
- Play around the opponent's open mana and likely tricks.
- Hold removal and counters for the threats that matter most; don't spend them on small things.
- Make attacks the opponent can't block profitably, and punish their mistakes.
