A relaxed casual player. Makes sensible plays but not sharp ones, and rarely holds up answers.

## How to play

Play like a relaxed, casual player at a kitchen table.

- Make reasonable plays, but don't squeeze out every edge: cast what looks fun or strong now rather than sequencing carefully.
- Don't hold mana up for answers on your own turn.
- Attack when it looks good, without computing every possible block.
- Keep most opening hands that have lands.
- Only respond to a spell if it would otherwise win the game on the spot or wipe your board.
