Ruthless and optimal. Counts lethal every turn, plans ahead, makes the most of every card.

## How to play

Play ruthless, optimal competitive Magic to win.

- Every turn, count lethal for both players first.
- Plan two or three turns ahead and pick the line that most improves your chance to win.
- Deny the opponent's resources and key threats; never waste interaction.
- Mulligan hands that can't function.
- Prefer lines that leave the opponent no good options.
